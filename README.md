# Study-of-investment-in-stock-market-by-college-students
🌟 Extract (Summary)

This study explores how college students engage with the stock market, focusing on their awareness, investment habits, risk-taking behavior, challenges, and motivations. The survey includes 57 students, mostly aged 15–20, studying commerce-related courses like BAF, BMS, and B.Com.

📌 Key Extract Points:

🎓 84.2% students are aware of the stock market.

📱 Social media & YouTube (59.6%) are the biggest sources of investment knowledge.

💰 61.4% have invested, mostly through Mutual Funds (SIP) (66%).

🧠 68.4% are beginners, revealing a gap between awareness and real understanding.

⚠️ Major challenges include fear of loss (46.4%), lack of guidance (39.3%), and market volatility (39.3%).

📚 86% of students want stock market education included in college curriculum.

Overall, the extract shows rising interest in investments among youth, but also highlights a need for structured financial education.

📘 Learnings from the Project
🔍 1. Students are Interested but Lack Deep Knowledge

Most students know about the stock market but lack practical investment skills and understanding.

📊 2. Digital Platforms Are the Primary Teachers

YouTube & social media have become the biggest influencers of financial decisions among youth.

💡 3. Mutual Funds (SIP) are the Safest Starting Point

Students prefer SIPs because they are less risky than direct stocks or derivatives.

⚖️ 4. Risk Capacity is Mostly Moderate

Most students can handle medium risk, showing balanced behavior toward investments.

🧩 5. Challenges Prevent Active Participation

Fear of loss & lack of guidance stop many from investing confidently.

👨‍🏫 6. Strong Demand for Financial Education

Students clearly want stock market-related subjects in college.



⭐ Main Importance of the Study
🔥 1. Builds Financial Awareness in Youth

Helps students understand saving, investing, wealth creation, and financial responsibility.

📈 2. Encourages Early Investment Habits

Starting early gives compounding benefits and better long-term financial stability.

🧭 3. Identifies Gaps in Knowledge

Shows where students lack guidance, helping colleges create better financial programs.

🎓 4. Supports Curriculum Development

Useful for educational institutions to include practical financial literacy in studies.

🏦 5. Contributes to National Financial Inclusion

More educated investors → better market participation → stronger economy.

🏁Conclusion

The study concludes that college students show strong interest and growing involvement in the stock market, driven by curiosity, desire for extra income, and long-term wealth creation.

However, despite high awareness, their practical knowledge remains limited, showing a need for proper guidance, financial education, and training programs. Students mostly invest small amounts and choose safer options, indicating cautious behavior.

With 86% supporting stock market education, it becomes essential for colleges to integrate financial literacy programs, helping students become responsible and confident investors.

Ultimately, early investment exposure can build stable financial habits, reduce dependence, and contribute to economic growth.
